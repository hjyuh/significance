What this board does not establish

  • No row says whether a result is right. "Checked" describes artifacts — files hashed, code rebuilt — not arguments read.
  • A row with more text in it is not doing better than a row with less. The rows are not ranked and are not ordered by anything but their place in the release.
  • An empty row means nobody here has looked at that result yet, and nothing more than that.
  • The board's own as-of date is when the board was gone over; each row carries its own, which may be older.

Significance mechanically validates provenance and a narrow set of evidence predicates. It does not mechanically determine mathematical truth.
