For: general_mathematician

This paper pins down a small but stubborn Ramsey-type number: it shows 18 vertices are enough to force a large monochromatic near-clique in any red/blue edge coloring, matching a construction that shows 17 is not enough.

[editorial_inference, asserted by editor-mz]

Importance

Closes a gap between the previously known bounds of 17 and 19 for this Ramsey-type quantity, reported open since a 2019 survey. [source_quote, asserted by author-as]
