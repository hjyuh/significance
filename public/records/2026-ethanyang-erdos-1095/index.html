Main deduction

Modify L_k by adding small-prime factors and removing the top prime band, producing a modulus M with room for multipliers t. Lucas's criterion becomes forbidden residue conditions in three prime ranges. PNT bounds their density; truncated inclusion–exclusion with explicit CRT discrepancy produces a surviving t. Set n = Mt - 1 and use the least-witness definition to conclude the strict eventual lcm bound.

Paper §§2–5; Erdos1095/Main.lean · Check both witness inequalities and a single threshold for all sufficiently large k. · Significance’s interpretation · Significance editor · 2026-10-01
