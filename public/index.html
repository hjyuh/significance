What a record does not establish

  • No field or rendered element asserts that a claim is verified, correct, or refuted.
  • Evidence predicates are narrow: schema shape, attribution, execution receipts, append-only history.
  • The publication state describes this page, never the standing of the mathematics.

Significance mechanically validates provenance and a narrow set of evidence predicates. It does not mechanically determine mathematical truth.
