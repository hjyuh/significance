Ask for a claim to be tracked.

A record says what was claimed, who asserted it, what evidence exists, and what nobody has checked yet. It does not say whether the mathematics is right, and nothing in it ever will. If that is the thing you need, this is how to ask for it — you do not need to know any of this project's vocabulary to file the request. Putting it in plain words is our job.

Before you ask — records about other people

A record about a living person's claim is filed only after that person has been contacted. If they decline, no record is filed, and nothing anywhere in this project records that they were asked or that they said no: there is no decline list, in public, in private, or in maintainer notes. Aggregate counts of outreach may be published; individuals who said no never appear in them. This mirrors the outreach rules in the project's moderation policy, and it applies to requests as much as to anything the maintainers initiate themselves.

Asking about your own claim skips all of that. You are the person who would otherwise be contacted.

Three things we need

The claim
A link to where it actually lives — paper, preprint, repository, announcement, post. The most stable one, if it is in several places.
Your role
Author, reader, or something else. This changes what happens next rather than how the request is ranked: an author can attest to their own work, and a reader asking about someone else's claim starts the contact step above.
What to track
In your own words. Which statement matters, what evidence you know of, what you think nobody has checked.

Action — open an issue

Open a prefilled record request on GitHub — the three fields above, already labelled. Needs a GitHub account.

Action — send it by email

No contact address is configured for this build, so there is no email link to offer. Rather than link somewhere that goes nowhere, here is the message to send once an address exists — the same three fields, in the same order: 

Subject: Record request

Link to the claim:

Your role (author / reader / other):

What you want tracked:

Setting contact_email in data/site.yaml turns this into a mailto link. It is deliberately unset rather than guessed: a link to an invented address would let somebody believe they had asked when nobody received anything.

What filing a request does not do

  • It does not commit anyone to creating a record.
  • A request that goes nowhere is closed without a public judgement attached to it.
  • A record, if one is created, still asserts nothing about whether the mathematics is right.

Significance mechanically validates provenance and a narrow set of evidence predicates. It does not mechanically determine mathematical truth.
