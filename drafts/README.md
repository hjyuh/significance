# Editorial drafts

Files in `drafts/records/` are private working drafts assembled from public
sources. They are not part of the canonical record collection, are not included
in the deployed build, and do not imply that a named author participated in or
approved a Significance record.

For an ordinary claim by a living individual, move a draft into `records/` only
after the author requests the record or explicitly opts in, following
`docs/moderation.md`.
